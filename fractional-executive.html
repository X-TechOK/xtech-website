Questions

Fractional Executive FAQ

What does "fractional" actually mean?

You get a named executive who serves as your CIO or COO on a part-time, ongoing basis — typically a set number of hours or days per month. Same accountability and seat at your leadership table, a fraction of the full-time cost.

How is this different from hiring a consultant?

A consultant delivers a recommendation and leaves. A fractional executive owns outcomes over time: the roadmap, the vendor relationships, the budget discipline, and the results. It's a leadership role, not a report.

What size company is this for?

Typically businesses from about 10 to a few hundred employees — big enough that technology and operations decisions carry real consequences, not yet big enough to justify a full-time C-suite hire. Our executives' experience spans two-person firms to 20,000-employee multinationals, so nothing about your scale will be new to them.

Can a fractional CIO handle our compliance requirements?

Yes — our executives have guided organizations through CJIS, HIPAA, FERPA, COPPA, CCPA, and GDPR obligations, including records governance under national-security handling requirements. Compliance oversight is one of the most common reasons clients engage us.

How do engagements start and how flexible are they?

Every engagement starts with a free consultation and an assessment phase with defined deliverables — so you see the value before committing to an ongoing cadence. From there, hours scale up or down with your needs.
