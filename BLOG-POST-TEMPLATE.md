## A section heading

Normal paragraphs are just typed text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

- Bullet points start with a dash
- **Bold text** uses two stars on each side
- [Links look like this](https://x-tech.tv/services.html)

> A quote or key takeaway goes on a line that starts with ">".
